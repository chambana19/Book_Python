PREDICT, OPTIMIZE, DECIDE

Assigned: Week 9 (October 19, 2026)
Due and presented: Week 15 (December 7, 2026)

PURPOSE
Build one auditable decision workflow: evaluate a predictive model, use it
inside one justified optimization method, compare the result with one simpler
alternative, verify feasibility, and communicate a bounded recommendation.

REQUIRED CORE
- One prediction track: continuous regression OR go/no-go classification.
- One primary optimization family: linear, nonlinear gradient-based, OR heuristic.
- One compact comparison using a second family or transparent baseline.
- One genuine predict–optimize connection.

NOT REQUIRED
- Reimplementing all three optimization families in the final project.
- Six different plots or a mandatory animation.
- Treating a model's recommendation as an automatic design decision.
