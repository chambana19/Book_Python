PROJECT 2 RUBRIC — 100 POINTS

1. FORMULATION, DATA PREPARATION, FAMILY JUSTIFICATION, SENSITIVITY — 15
13–15: Variables, units, objective, constraints, bounds, baseline, the required
outlier check on the working dataset, sweep, and family choice form one coherent
problem.
10–12: Mostly coherent with minor ambiguity or limited sensitivity evidence.
0–9: Solver chosen before the problem is defined, units/bounds are missing, or no
outlier check was done.

2. EVALUATED PREDICTION AND MODEL CARD — 20
18–20: Leakage-free fit/validation/final-test protocol; model and threshold choices
use no final-test labels; suitable baseline; correct untouched-test metrics/diagnostic;
manual check; calibration/uncertainty status, domain, and refusal rule are explicit.
14–17: Working evaluation with incomplete checking or interpretation.
0–13: Training-only evidence, leakage, unsupported accuracy claim, or missing card.

3. PRIMARY OPTIMIZATION AND CONSTRAINT VERIFICATION — 20
18–20: Family fits the formulation; settings/tolerances/objective-call accounting are
recorded; result is reproducible; status and every constraint are independently checked;
the solver card distinguishes a mathematical guarantee from a best-found result.
14–17: Correct solution with limited verification or justification.
0–13: Infeasible/unchecked result or method–problem mismatch.

4. COMPACT COMPARISON — 15
13–15: Transparent second method/baseline uses the same bounded sub-problem and
compares objective, feasibility, runtime, and sensitivity fairly.
10–12: Comparison works but is narrow or unevenly documented.
0–9: Disconnected example, incomparable settings, or no verification.

5. PREDICT–OPTIMIZE–DECIDE INTEGRATION — 20
18–20: Evaluated frozen model artifact is genuinely used inside the search; marginal
and joint domain guards are active; baseline and recommendation are compared using a
defensible uncertainty method; both receive documented non-surrogate confirmation.
14–17: Connection exists but limits, domain, or verification are incomplete.
0–13: Prediction and optimization remain disconnected, recommendation extrapolates,
or a surrogate-only optimum is presented as a confirmed design decision.

6. COMMUNICATION, REPRODUCIBILITY, LIMITS, AND AI RECORD — 10
9–10: Four figures, report, presentation, README, cards, and disclosure create a
clear evidence trail and distinguish recommendation from decision.
7–8: Understandable with minor gaps.
0–6: Missing files, broken execution, unclear argument, or incomplete disclosure.
