PROBLEM / DATE / STUDENT

PRIMARY FAMILY AND JUSTIFICATION
Linear, bounded nonlinear, or heuristic; explain why it fits the variables,
objective, and constraints.

DESIGN VARIABLES, UNITS, AND BOUNDS

OBJECTIVE AND CONSTRAINTS
State direction, equations/logic, and units.

SETTINGS
Library and version; method; tolerances/options; initial guess or random seed;
population/iteration limits; stopping rule; objective-call definition and budget.

STATUS AND RESULT
Success/status code and complete message; objective value; candidate values; runtime;
nit/nfev or measured objective calls. State numerical feasibility tolerance.

FEASIBILITY CHECK
List every constraint value, limit, residual/slack, and pass/fail.

COMPARISON METHOD
Second family or transparent baseline, common sub-problem, result and runtime.

SENSITIVITY / STABILITY
Initial guesses, repeated seeds with distribution, bounds, or parameter changes tested.

GUARANTEE AND SUPPORT
What is mathematically guaranteed for the encoded problem, what is only best-found,
and whether the candidate passed featurewise and joint model-support checks.

LIMITATIONS AND NEXT VERIFICATION
Name the independent simulation/measurement used for both baseline and candidate,
or mark the decision NOT READY. An optimizer success flag is not external validation.
