SOURCE-DECK POLICY
  There is no slide-count cap. Each lecture retains the complete authored
  progression, worked examples, practice prompts, visual models, and optional
  2026 practice-horizon material. Decks therefore have different slide
  counts and some intentionally exceed the nominal 80-minute meeting if every
  optional slide is shown. The instructor controls delivery by hiding,
  skipping, or shortening selected slides; source decks are not cut merely to
  reach a uniform number.

SUPERSEDED OUTPUTS
  No _Archive_Superseded folder exists in this package. Historical planning
  and completion records remain only as explicitly labeled provenance files;
  the folders above and the generated course_manifest.yml define the current
  distribution.

NAMING RULE
  Each deck and handout uses the week in which it is taught. Week 12 is
  heuristic/metaheuristic optimization. Week 13 is evaluated-prediction
  machine learning (regression). Week 14 is classification/model cards, and
  Week 15 has one capstone meeting only. Student handouts use QUESTION,
  INPUTS/ASSUMPTIONS, METHOD, and CHECKS/INTERPRET headings in every # %% cell.
