## Use during teaching

- Treat every presentation as a complete source deck. Slide counts are topic-driven and intentionally differ; hide or skip locally when pacing requires it.
- The transfer item is a 3–5 minute check, not a new graded deliverable.
- Ask for a prediction before students run code or inspect a solver result.
- Require units, a known-answer/boundary/feasibility check, and one limitation.
- Accept generative-AI assistance only when the student can explain the logic, test it, and record what was accepted, modified, or rejected.
- Preserve the sequence: introduce the principle in Meeting A, realize it in Spyder during Meeting B, and connect the result to a bounded architectural decision.
