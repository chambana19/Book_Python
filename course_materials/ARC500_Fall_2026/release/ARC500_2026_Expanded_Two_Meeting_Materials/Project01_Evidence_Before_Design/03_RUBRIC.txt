PROJECT 1 RUBRIC — 100 POINTS

1. QUESTION, PROVENANCE, CLEANING, AND CHECKS — 20
18–20: Question is focused; row meaning, source, and units are explicit; checks
and cleaning decisions are reproducible; cleaned export is correct.
14–17: Mostly complete with minor gaps or weak justification.
0–13: Question/data mismatch, missing provenance, or unreliable cleaning.

2. GROUPED EVIDENCE AND OUTLIER DECISION — 20
18–20: Groups are meaningful; summary is correct; IQR flags are verified; every
flag has a defensible written decision and the conclusion is sensitivity-aware.
14–17: Correct basic analysis with incomplete interpretation or checking.
0–13: Unsupported grouping, incorrect flags, or silent deletion of evidence.

3. TWO STATIC FIGURES — 20
18–20: Both figures answer distinct questions, use appropriate encodings, label
units, and expose an important pattern or consequence of cleaning/outliers.
14–17: Readable and mostly purposeful with minor design or interpretation gaps.
0–13: Decorative, duplicated, misleading, or not reproducible.

4. MAP OR ANIMATION EXTENSION — 15
13–15: Correct method, explicit assumptions, readable output, and a clear reason
the extension changes or qualifies the claim.
10–12: Technically adequate but weakly connected to the argument.
0–9: Incorrect CRS/animation logic, missing output, or decorative use.

5. INTERPRETATION AND BOUNDED RECOMMENDATION — 15
13–15: Claim matches the evidence, distinguishes observation from decision, names
at least two limitations, and proposes a useful next step.
10–12: Mostly supported but too broad or incomplete on limits.
0–9: Causal/absolute claim unsupported by the data.

6. REPRODUCIBILITY, CODE CLARITY, README, AND AI RECORD — 10
9–10: Package runs from the project folder; files are clearly named; functions,
comments, README, data dictionary, and disclosure make the work auditable.
7–8: Runs with small intervention or minor documentation gaps.
0–6: Broken paths, missing files, opaque code, or incomplete disclosure.
