ARC 500 — PROJECT 1 BRIEF
EVIDENCE BEFORE DESIGN: AN ARCHITECTURAL DATA STORY

PROJECT QUESTION
What can a small, documented architectural dataset support us in claiming,
and where does that claim stop?

Use the course building-room and zone datasets, or an instructor-approved
alternative, to answer one specific question. Examples include:
- Which orientation or program group has the highest energy use per area?
- Which rooms deserve investigation after an outlier audit?
- How does a performance measure vary by zone or room type?
- Where does a mapped spatial condition support a limited design response?

PART I — DATA AS EVIDENCE
- State the question in one sentence.
- Record the source, collection/generation method, units, and what one row means.
- Inspect shape, column names, dtypes, missing values, duplicates, and ranges.
- Clean only with written reasons.
- Export cleaned_data.csv with index=False.

PART II — GROUPING AND OUTLIER DECISION
- Compare at least two meaningful groups.
- Export grouped_summary.csv with group, n, mean, median, and outlier_count.
- Apply the IQR rule to one outcome.
- Keep missing_flag and outlier_flag separate: a missing outcome has no value to
  compare with an IQR fence and must not be counted as an outlier.
- For every flagged row, record KEEP, INVESTIGATE, or EXCLUDE and explain why.
- A z-score comparison or merge is an extension, not a core requirement.

PART III — TWO STATIC FIGURES
- Figure 1: a distribution or grouped-comparison figure.
- Figure 2: a relationship or change figure.
- Use fig, ax = plt.subplots(...), label axes and units, and save at 200 dpi.
- At least one figure must reveal the effect of cleaning or the outlier decision.
- Annotate one important row, group, or turning point.
- course_style.py is provided in Starter_Code/: call its apply_house_style(ax,
  title=..., xlabel=..., ylabel=..., source_note=...) on every figure for one
  consistent look. You call this function; you do not need to edit it.

PART IV — CHOOSE ONE EXTENSION
Choose A or B, not both:
A. MAP: state the CRS, reproject before measuring, complete one spatial operation,
   and export one map PNG.
B. ANIMATION: show change across time, category, or a parameter and export one GIF.

FINAL CLAIM
Write one bounded recommendation supported by the table and figures. Name at
least two limitations and one next evidence-gathering step.

DELIVERABLES
1. analysis.py and optional evidence.py.
2. cleaned_data.csv and grouped_summary.csv.
3. two static PNG figures plus one map PNG OR one animation GIF.
4. A 600–800-word illustrated evidence memo.
5. README, data dictionary, and AI-use record.
6. A six-slide pin-up: cover plus five content slides.

AI USE
Generative AI may help with code. You remain responsible for the question,
